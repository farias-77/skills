# Writing an artifact

Every artifact is in English: the report's Video, Deck and Explainer,
and every page, deck or film a session asks for, with the exceptions
under "What stays as it is". The chat with the user stays in the user's
language. The English is simplified, about 80% of
ASD-STE100, because the reader may read English as a second language.

## Words

- **One word, one meaning.** Use the same word for the same thing every
  time. Never use a synonym for variety.

  | Say | It means | Never say |
  |---|---|---|
  | workstream | a unit of work: one demand, from the idea to production | front |
  | frontend | the screens and the browser code | front, FE |
  | backend | the server, the API and the data | back, BE |

  Other terms are the ones in `claude/GLOSSARY.md`. Define any other term
  at its first use.
- **The short word.** Use `use`, `start`, `make sure`, `before`, `about`,
  `to`. Do not use `utilize`, `commence`, `ensure`, `prior to`,
  `approximately`, `in order to`, `leverage`, `facilitate`.
- **Three nouns in a row at most.** "the entry gate result file" becomes
  "the result file of the entry gate".
- **Technical names stay exact:** paths, commands, field names, and agent
  names with their model and effort.

## Sentences

- One idea in each sentence, 20 words at most.
- Active voice: the actor comes first. "The gate stops the merge", not
  "the merge is stopped".
- Simple tenses: "runs", not "is running"; "merged", not "has merged".
- Keep the small words: "the", "a", "this" and the verb. Do not chain
  clauses with colons, `·` or arrows. Arrows belong in diagrams.
- The condition comes first: "If the gate is red, …".
- A warning gives the command first, then the risk.

## Paragraphs

- One topic in each paragraph, the topic sentence first, six sentences
  at most.
- Three or more items go in a list.

## What stays as it is

- A verbatim quote stays in its own language.
- The close's video for users (every word on screen) and the "what's
  new" text, also on its Close deck slide, use the users' language:
  they are product copy, like the product they show.
- Identifiers, paths and file names.
